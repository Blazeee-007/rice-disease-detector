Deep Learning Architecture

The system leverages MobileNetV2, a lightweight Convolutional Neural Network architecture built on inverted residual blocks and depthwise separable convolutions. Transfer learning initialized from ImageNet weights allows accurate feature extraction from leaf veins, discoloration margins, and spore lesions without requiring tens of thousands of newly captured samples.
