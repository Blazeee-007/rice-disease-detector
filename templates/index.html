Why Detect Rice Leaf Disease?

Rice leaf diseases affect crop yield and grain quality. Bacterial blight, leaf blast, and brown spot can decimate 10% to 50% of the entire field if undetected.

Early and accurate detection enables timely treatment with precise bactericides/fungicides, preventing widespread contagion, saving precious resources, and maximizing harvest productivity.

Prevent Rice leaf Disease:
  1. Use clean tools — Disinfect farming implements between plots.
  2. Inspect plants regularly — Scout leaves weekly during tillering.
  3. Use quality soil and fertilizers — Avoid nitrogen overdosing.
  4. Ensure good air circulation — Maintain recommended hill spacing.
  5. Remove infected leaves — Collect and burn infected plant debris.
