Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD)

Continuous ponding of water is not mandatory and creates ideal conditions for fungal spores (Blast and Brown Spot). AWD reduces irrigation water by up to 30% while maintaining equivalent crop yield.

  • Keep 2–5 cm water layer during first 2 weeks after transplanting.
  • Allow water level to naturally recede 15 cm below ground before re-irrigation.
  • Keep flooded during flowering (day 60 to 80) to avoid panicle sterility.
Nutrient Management Schedule (NPK)

Properly timed split application prevents nutrient leaching and builds physiological resistance to foliar pathogen entry.

  • Basal Dose (Transplanting): Full dose of Phosphorus (P) + 50% Potassium (K) + 25% Nitrogen (N).
  • Active Tillering (Day 25): 50% Nitrogen (N) top-dressing.
  • Panicle Initiation (Day 55): 25% Nitrogen (N) + remaining 50% Potassium (K).
  • Zinc Application: 25 kg/ha ZnSO₄ basal dose to avert khaira disease.
